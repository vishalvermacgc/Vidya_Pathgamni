Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Find quick answers to the most common questions about our platform.

How do I enroll in a course?
Simply create a free account, browse our course catalog, and click "Enroll" on any course that interests you. Payment is only required for premium courses.
Are the courses self-paced?
Yes! All our courses are designed for flexible learning. Study at your own pace, revisit lessons anytime, and complete assignments on your schedule.
Do I get a certificate upon completion?
Absolutely! Every course completion earns you a digital certificate that you can share on LinkedIn, add to your resume, or showcase to employers.
How do I become a teacher on the platform?
Sign up as a teacher, complete our quick instructor verification, and start creating your first course. We provide all the tools and support you need.
What are the fees for teachers?
Creating and publishing courses is completely free. We only take a small commission (15%) on course sales to cover platform costs and support.
Is there customer support available?
Yes! Our support team is available 24/7 via email, live chat, and our comprehensive help center. We're here to help you succeed.
Can I access courses on mobile devices?
Definitely! Our platform is fully responsive and works seamlessly on smartphones, tablets, and desktops. Learn anywhere, anytime.
What if I'm not satisfied with a course?
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid courses. If you're not happy, we'll refund your payment — no questions asked.
